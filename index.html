FAQ

Common Questions

Answers about our pool cleaning service, coverage area, and how quotes work.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Dallas, Rowlett, Plano, Murphy, McKinney, Frisco, Sachse, Garland, Richardson, Carrollton, Wylie, and Rockwall. In Dallas we regularly work in Preston Hollow, Lakewood, Lake Highlands, Oak Lawn, Uptown, and North Dallas.

What is included in a weekly pool cleaning visit?

Every weekly visit follows the same routine: skim the surface and remove leaves and debris, brush the walls, steps, and tile line, vacuum the pool floor, empty the skimmer and pump baskets, test and balance water chemistry, inspect the pump, filter, and equipment, and send you photos with a detailed service report.

Will I know what happened at my pool while I was away?

Yes. After every service visit we send photos and a detailed report of what we did and what we found, including the water, the chemistry readings, and any issues we spotted, so you never have to guess.

Are you insured and licensed?

We are fully insured, so your property is protected on every visit. We hold a Certified Pool Operator certification (CPO C-163589) and a Texas Residential Appliance Installer License (RAIL 335062), so equipment work is done to code by qualified hands.

How does the quote work?

We give a free written quote after seeing your situation, with no pressure. That includes a free walkthrough of your pool, no hidden fees, and a clear list of what is included.

My pool turned green. Can you fix it?

Yes. Our Green to Clean Pool Service turns green, algae-filled pools back to blue with a proven multi-step treatment.

Do you repair and install pool equipment?

We do. We offer fast diagnosis and repair for pumps, motors, and valves that stop working, plus professional installation of pumps, filters, heaters, and salt systems built to last.

Can I change my service later?

Yes. Once service starts you get the same routine every visit, photos and reports after each service, and you can adjust anytime as your needs change.
